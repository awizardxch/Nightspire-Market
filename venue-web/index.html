Offer board

Nightspire Market is a read-only venue board. It never holds keys, never signs, and never submits transactions. To fill an offer, copy the offer terms and sign in your own wallet — see how fills work.

Reads venue-api/openapi.yaml endpoints only: GET /v1/offers, GET /v1/offers/{offerId}, GET /v1/auctions/{auctionId}.
